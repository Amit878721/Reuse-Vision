Model background and published benchmark (reference)

The public model card describes a YOLO11n detector trained for four motherboard-part classes. The publisher reports validation precision 88.9%, recall 76.1%, mAP@50 82.7%, and mAP@50–95 55.9%. These are the publisher's reported results, not measurements produced by this app and not independently reproduced here.

Model classes: CPU Socket, PCIe Slot, Power Connector, and RAM Slot. It does not claim to detect complete CPU, GPU, RAM sticks, HDD, SSD, or PSU units.
